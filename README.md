### Solang Valley

Solang Valley is one of the most popular destinations near Manali. Surrounded by impressive mountain landscapes, it attracts visitors interested in sightseeing, outdoor activities, and adventure experiences.

Old Manali offers a more relaxed and traditional side of the destination. Its cafés, small shops, winding lanes, and mountain atmosphere make it popular with travelers who want to explore beyond the main tourist center.

A visit to Old Manali can easily be combined with a stay around Hamta.

For travelers who enjoy short hikes and natural attractions, Jogini Waterfalls is another popular place to visit.

The walk toward the waterfall provides an opportunity to experience forested surroundings and Himalayan scenery while enjoying a relatively short outdoor excursion.

The Hamta region itself is one of the main reasons to choose this part of Manali.

The valley provides beautiful mountain scenery and opportunities to enjoy nature at a slower pace. Travelers interested in trekking and more adventurous experiences can also explore routes leading toward the higher Himalayan landscapes.

## Best Time for a Dome Stay in Manali

Manali offers something different in every season.

**Summer** is popular for sightseeing, road trips, trekking, and exploring the surrounding valleys. The weather is generally pleasant compared with many parts of India.

**Monsoon** brings greenery to the mountains and creates a fresh landscape, although travelers should keep changing weather conditions in mind when planning outdoor activities.

**Autumn** can be an excellent period for travelers looking for comfortable weather and clearer mountain views.

**Winter** transforms the region with snow and attracts visitors who want to experience a classic snowy Himalayan holiday.

The best time ultimately depends on whether you prefer greenery, outdoor exploration, comfortable weather, or snow-covered landscapes.
